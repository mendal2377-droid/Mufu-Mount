# Sound credits — Mufu: Changing Skies

This is an edited, layered sound design. The recordings below were not made at Mount Mufu. Excerpts were trimmed, looped/crossfaded, gain-adjusted and mixed in stereo; bird samples were panned and thunder was placed after animated lightning. Stone/asphalt footsteps are original synthesized Foley.

- **Ambient Mountain, River, Wind and Forest and Waterfall**, Blender Foundation / Yo Frankie!, submitted by Lamoot. Forest and wind excerpts. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-mountain-river-wind-and-forest-and-waterfall). [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
- **Forest bird sounds**, pauliuw. Individual recorded bird samples. [Source](https://opengameart.org/content/forest-bird-sounds). [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **Waves.ogg**, Dsw4. Lake Ontario shore recordings, used as a broad-water ambience substitute. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Waves.ogg). Released into the public domain by the author.
- **Rain and thunder.ogg**, Caesar. Recorded in Sundbyberg, Sweden. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_and_thunder.ogg). Released into the public domain by the author.
- **Walking through snow.mp3**, Lukas Beck. Recorded in Berlin, Germany. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Walking_through_snow.mp3). [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

No creator endorsement is implied. Keep this credit file with copies of the video or soundtrack. The video metadata and closing card also identify the credited contributors.
