## How Tiny Nanjing uses this

**Avenues by name:**
- Plane on the roads listed above.
- Elsewhere, street trees are drawn by census share: camphor 26 %, plane 17 %, and the rest split among scholar tree, privet, metasequoia, cedar and wingnut. The split of the rest is an **assumption**, weighted to the 2007 dominant list.

**Places:**
- Cherries along Jiming Temple road and in Xuanwu Lake's cherry areas.
- Ginkgo at Qingliangshan, the Xuanwu ginkgo avenue (梁洲), the two university campuses and the Ming Xiaoling Sacred Way.
- Plum at Meihua Shan and on 梁洲.
- Willows on lake shores.
- Woods (北极阁, 幕府山) as mixed broadleaf with conifers.

**Seasons follow the phenology table:**
- plum in late February, cherry in late March, ginkgo gold in late November;
- metasequoia red in early December, plane yellow from late November;
- camphor and cedar evergreen.
