# Qixia Mountain (research; not built yet)

- **Relic pagoda 舍利塔:** five storeys, eight-sided, stone, about 18 m (18.73 m). Rebuilt in stone under the Southern Tang. Carved bodhisattvas and heavenly kings; the base has reliefs of the eight scenes of the Buddha's life. Sources: [Architectura Sinica](https://architecturasinica.org/place/000294a), [ctrip](https://you.ctrip.com/sight/nanjing9/1671.html).
- **Thousand Buddha Cliff 千佛岩:** north-east of the temple beside the pagoda, with 515 to 700 figures (sources differ). The Great Buddha niche (489 CE) holds an Amitayus Buddha about 12 m tall.
- **Autumn:** red maples on the hillside; a red boardwalk through maples (owner's photos `D:\blender\nanjing\qixia`).
