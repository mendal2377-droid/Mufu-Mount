# Niushou Mountain (research; not built yet)

- **Usnisha Palace 佛顶宫:** a large shell dome ("the kasaya") draped over a smaller dome (a lotus base topped by a mani jewel). Overall about 220 × 160 m, 89.3 m high. Built in a disused quarry pit. Inside are nine levels (three above ground, six below); the Chanjing Grand View is an ellipse 112 × 62 m, 41.2 m clear height, with a reclining Buddha. Sources: [chinanews 2015](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/m/cul/2015/10-27/7592182.shtml), [ctrip](https://you.ctrip.com/sight/nanjing9/18093.html), [thepaper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_1389923).
- **Foding Pagoda 佛顶塔:** nine storeys, four sides, Tang style. Height 88 m in most travel sources; one PDF says 68 m. Use 88 m and note the conflict.
- **Look:** gold lattice dome beside a rose-gold shell, a reflecting pool, the dark-red pagoda with gold roofs on a pale plaza (owner's photos `D:\blender\nanjing\niushoumount`).

## As built (blender/build_niushou.py)

Local frame: metres from the Usnisha Palace (OSM way 1160624650). The OSM oval (106 × 154 m) holds the small gold dome on a cream arcaded drum, ringed by a moat. The lattice robe is a vault 58 m high at its mouth, which faces the plaza, tapering to a tail to the north-east; its plan is assumed from the photographs. The pagoda follows OSM (a 42 m podium turned 20°) and is 88 m from the plaza: a 4 m podium plus 84 m of tower. The quarry pit is filled to the plaza level, and the hills are the DSM with 6 m of canopy taken off.
